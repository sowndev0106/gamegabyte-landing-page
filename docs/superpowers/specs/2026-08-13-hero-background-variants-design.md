# Hero Background Variants Design

## Goal

Create three distinct 16:9 raster backgrounds for the Gamegabyte landing-page hero. The user will compare them and select one for later integration.

## Shared visual language

- Premium dark sci-fi gaming art aligned with the existing black/ink interface.
- Purple and ultraviolet energy as the main accent, with restrained acid-lime highlights matching `#b6e802`.
- Cinematic depth, crisp silhouettes, and polished game key-art quality.
- Keep the central headline and CTA zone dark, low-contrast, and visually quiet.
- Compose safely for `object-cover` across desktop and mobile crops.
- No text, typography, logos, UI, borders, signatures, or watermarks.

## Variants

### 1. Cyber heroine

A futuristic female game character occupies the right third, backed by a purple energy ring. Her face and armor remain outside the central copy zone. This is the most dramatic, character-led option and is closest to the supplied hero reference.

### 2. Portal arena

An unoccupied futuristic arena or ruin frames a luminous purple portal. Architectural shapes lead inward while the center remains dark enough for white and lime copy. This is the most flexible option for responsive crops.

### 3. Fantasy-sci-fi battlefield

A wide atmospheric environment with a small distant warrior silhouette, subtle floating debris, and restrained purple/lime energy. The scale feels epic without turning the character into the primary subject.

## Output

- Generate each variant independently with the built-in image generation tool.
- Save all accepted outputs under `public/assets/img/backgrounds/` with unique filenames.
- Inspect each result for composition, palette, copy clearance, unwanted text, and obvious generation artifacts.
- Do not change the active hero asset reference until the user chooses a winner.
