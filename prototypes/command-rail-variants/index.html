Twenty rails, one job

Every variant carries the same twelve sections, the wordmark and the status readout — only the marker language changes. Scrub the active section below to see how each one behaves under movement, then open any of them full-page to judge it against real scroll. Two of them are families rather than one-offs: 08 and 11–15 are six ways for a dial to report a heading, 09 and 16–20 are six ways to read the edge of a strip of film.
